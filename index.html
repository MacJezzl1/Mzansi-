📖 HOW TO PLAY
✕

🎯 Objective

1
Capture cards from the table. Score points for most cards (3pts), most spades (1pt), Aces (1pt each), 2♠ Big Casino (1pt), 10♦ (2pts), sweeps (1pt each).

🃏 Basic Actions

1
DECLARE: Tap a hand card — stays in hand, sets your target value.
2
CAPTURE: Tap table cards or a build equalling your card's value, then hit Capture.
3
BUILD: Tap contrib card from hand (teal) + table cards summing to declared value. No builds in Round 1.
4
EXTEND: Add more cards to your existing build. Declared card stays in hand.
5
PLACE: Play your declared card onto the table. Last resort.

🌍 Original Mzansi Rules

★
INKOSI Ace of Spades is the Chief. Capturing it = +3 bonus points.
★
UBUNTU BUILD Use 5+ cards in one build move = bonus sweep point. Unity is strength.
★
BRAAI TAX 8+ cards on table? Cannot PLACE — must capture or build.
★
WILD CARD One purple card per game counts as any value for builds.

🆕 New Features v5

🔮
SANGOMA SACRED One table card pulses purple each round — capture it for +2 bonus pts.
🎺
VUVUZELA MODE 3 consecutive captures = Vuvuzela! Button turns gold. Next capture = DOUBLE score.
😤
EISH COUNTER When Zola sweeps, an EISH! button flashes for 5 seconds. Tap it for a coin-flip challenge — win = sweep cancelled!
🐘
NDLOVU TROPHY Collect all four 10s = +5 bonus pts at game end. Track them above your hand.
👻
GHOST CARD One ghosted card = any value can capture it. Normal capture rules skipped.
⚡
LIGHTNING ROUND Every 3rd round is Lightning. All builds that round score +1 pt each at game end.
🎲
KASI DICE Each round a dice rolls at the start. High (4–6) = your builds +1 this round. Low (1–3) = Zola's builds +1.
⚡
TOWNSHIP HUSTLE When Zola is forced to place, you earn 1 coin per table card. Hustle the hustle.
💰
BLESSER CARD Have the 5♦ in your pile when game ends = +3 bonus pts.
🤝
UBUNTU CHAIN Extend the same build 3 times in one game = all your builds scored ×2.
👑
INKOSI GUARD While holding Ace♠ in hand, Zola cannot steal your builds.

⚡ Power-Ups (once per game each)

🔮
Peek (50🪙): See Zola's full hand for 3 seconds.
✊
Toyi-Toyi (75🪙): Force Zola to place her weakest card next turn.
🛡
Ubuntu Shield (60🪙): Protect your builds from capture for 1 turn.
🍖
Braai Break (90🪙): Zola skips her next turn.
💍
Lobola Swap (40🪙): Swap one hand card with one table card.
👺
Tokoloshe Trap (65🪙): Trap a table card — if Zola captures it, steal 2 of her captured cards back.
🌿
Sangoma Vision (55🪙): Flip the top 2 deck cards face-up onto the table for free info.

🫳 Stealing from AI

1
Tap Zola's pile counter to reveal her captured cards.
2
Tap cards in the spread to mark them STEAL (red highlight).
3
Use them in a BUILD or EXTEND — they get consumed from her pile.
